This corpus contains three Amis texts from:

> Tamih and Montgomery, Robert L. (1962) Amis text. Work Papers of the Summer Institute of Linguistics, University of North Dakota Session: Vol. 6, Article 6. 10.31356/silwp.vol06.06

The 37 numbered examples and their lexical word glosses were checked against the source scan. The three printed titles are headings, not sentences, so they are not encoded as S elements. The printed form `mi-salrama` is retained.

Surviving public TEXT, S, and W identifiers are preserved. Standard FORM tiers copy the historical source transcription because no source-specific standardization profile is approved. Earlier unpinned PHON tiers are omitted.

The exact source inventory, source-owned records, ID ledger, reproduction scripts, and QC evidence are in `CodeAndDocs/`. The authoritative private source revision is recorded in `source_manifest.json`.
