Quality Control Modifications

Below is a list of preliminary lexical adjustments made to Siraya words broked up by line breaks in the text by hyphen -. 
They have been consolidated based on full word appearances in surrounding verses. 
Main QC adjustments include hyphen deletions, unnecessary space deletions, fixing mis-capitalized letters, and obvious mispellings. 
Every second letter due to typeface was made lowercase for consistency's sake. 
S's and f's were often counfounded, as well as vowels such as â, æ, ä, and é (for example, OCR Réni instead of Râni). 
The other main contirbution of QC was adding missing verses: 
The XML files leave out all final verses of a chapter if a new chapter starts on the same page; these were hand-transcribed. 
Around halfway through the files, there was around a 50-verse jump that misaligned the chapters. 
I re-aligned the correct verses with the correct chapters and deleted the 22nd chapter file (there are only 21). 
I also left, as comments at the top of each XML file, the chapter number and subtitle in Siraya.
