# Source evidence

The authoritative source is the [ANU Open Research Repository item](https://openresearch-repository.anu.edu.au/items/8bfb8bf0-2f58-4eae-947c-bf9af50faf9f)
for the 12-ICAL volume. The article occupies PDF pages 394–402 and printed
pages 401–409.

The source's printed page 401 identifies the article as CC BY 4.0. The reviewed
ledger records every included example's page locator, source form, translation,
gloss, and review note. The XML contains all 24 numbered examples and the three
Thao examples in footnote 7. Pages containing only prose, a frequency table, or
references contribute no sentence records.

The committed `reviewed_examples.tsv` is the deterministic build input. No
private source files, downloaded audio, credentials, or source PDF are needed
to reproduce the XML.
