What to do

These are lines from 博利亞潘世光阿漢字典 (O Citig no Amis, Cuerq & Pourrias 2002), an Amis–Chinese dictionary. When the book was typed up, each of these printed lines was marked as blank by mistake, so its text is missing. Each card shows a strip of the page scan, five printed lines, with the missing line among them.

  • Find the line. The card gives the text of the line just above and just below the missing one. The missing line is the one between them in the scan. Don't rely on its position in the strip; the strips are sometimes cut one line off.
  • Type it exactly as printed: the Amis, the Chinese, and all punctuation. Use ’ or ' for the Amis apostrophe (either is fine), - for a dash, =, and the braces {S}, {N}, {J} etc. as printed. For the colon between the Amis and the Chinese, type :. Keep the book's spelling even if it looks odd.
  • A line can be part of a longer entry (it may start in the middle of a sentence). Type just that printed line.
  • If the line is really blank (white space, a picture, a page edge), choose Really blank. If you can't read part of it, type what you can, put [?] where you can't, and say so in the note.
  • Mark done when the card is finished.
