# Dialect evidence

The corpus is labeled `dialect="Coastal"`.

This is a source-provenance label, not an automatic classification of every
dictionary form:

- An interview with author Safolu Kacaw (蔡中涵) describes his dictionary work in
  the Madawdaw community in Chenggong:
  https://ngiha-mag.com/special-site/tracingrootsrecordoforigins/dialogue/amis-moedict
- The Council of Indigenous Peoples' official community index places Madawdaw
  (麻荖漏部落, Chenggong Township) in its Coastal Amis (海岸阿美語) index:
  https://www.cip.gov.tw/data/news/202107/T-08726378.pdf?c=60B90646E1DF2081272C65C8ADCEBB89
- The Taiwan Indigenous Peoples Encyclopedia independently identifies
  Madawdaw's language as Coastal Amis:
  https://aborgpedia.alcd.center/detail?cat=0&id=11497&race=3

The dictionary is large and may contain broader Amis material. `Coastal`
therefore records the author's documented community and language variety; it
does not claim that dialect membership was inferred independently for all
49,000-plus examples.
