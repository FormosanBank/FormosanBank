Already settled; shown so you can object

Senses follow POL-025. In a dictionary headword entry, each sense after the first is a ver="alt" TRANSL. One TRANSL per sense, as in ILRDF's *_dictionary.xml files. What counts as one headword is not settled: version 1 of this page merged words spelled alike into one S. The first card for each corpus now asks whether homophones get their own S, with what the source's structure can and cannot tell apart. (Blust and Cuerq-Pourrias use one S per sense instead; I am not touching them.)
