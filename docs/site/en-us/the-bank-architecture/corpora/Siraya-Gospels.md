# Siraya Gospels of Gravius

Daniël Gravius and Anthonius Hambrouck's 1661 *Het Heylige Euangelium Matthei en Johannis* contains Matthew and John in Siraya alongside Dutch. This corpus publishes the printed Siraya verse columns with aligned public-domain English and Mandarin reference translations.

The corpus has 49 XML files and 1,951 sentence records: 1,071 verse units from Matthew and 880 from John. Every Siraya form was reviewed against 314 rendered scan pages. A full source ledger records the printed locator and page range for each sentence, while chapter-level and corpus-level hashes protect the completed review.

The Dutch parallel columns and book or chapter headings are outside the reviewed verse-corpus scope. Earlier Dutch OCR was incomplete and unreliable, so it is not retained as corpus data.

## Translations and versification

All 1,951 sentence records have an aligned King James Bible translation. The Chinese Union Version supplies 1,947 Mandarin translations; four verses absent from that edition are documented in the public source ledger.

Gravius divides modern John 1:38 into two printed verse units. The English and Mandarin reference text is partitioned across source verses 38 and 39 without rewriting it. From source verse 40 onward, the source numbering is one greater than modern editions through the end of John 1.

## Orthography and reproduction

The `original` FORM preserves the historical printed spelling, punctuation, spacing, diacritics, and hyphenation. FormosanBank currently designates no Siraya standard orthography and has no Siraya phonology profile, so this corpus intentionally has no standard FORM or PHON tier.

All 49 published text IDs and all 1,951 sentence IDs are preserved. The public structured source snapshot, pinned translation inputs, focused correction rules, source-review manifests, ledger, and generator reproduce the XML deterministically.
