Rau and Dong

This corpus contains 20 glossed texts from Rau and Dong (2006).

The xml's for each story can be found at: https://github.com/FormosanBank/FormosanBank/tree/main/Corpora/RauDong/XML 

The citation is Rau, D. V., and Dong, M. N. (2006). Yami texts with reference grammar and dictionary. Institute of Linguistics, Academia Sinica.
