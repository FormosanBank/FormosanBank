# Truku Word Formation — Hsu (2008) Thesis

This corpus is drawn from Lowking Wei-Cheng Hsu's (許韋晟) 2008 MA thesis, 太魯閣語構詞法研究 (*Word Formation in Truku*), submitted to the Graduate Institute of Taiwan Languages and Language Education, National Hsin-Chu University of Education. The thesis's numbered Truku example sentences were extracted from the source PDF (via a text-layer extraction pipeline, no OCR), paired with their Mandarin translations, and — where the source's interlinear gloss line aligns reliably — annotated with word- and morpheme-level glosses. The Truku is recorded in its source Ortho94 orthography (with FormosanBank's standard Ortho113 tier alongside). The data is used by FormosanBank with the author's permission.

## **Access Details**

* The repo containing this corpus in FormosanBank as well as the code to reconstruct the corpus can be found [here](https://github.com/FormosanBank/FormosanBank/tree/main/Corpora/Nowbucyang-Truku-Thesis).
* The XML is rebuilt reproducibly by `CodeAndDocs/generate_xml.sh`; the thesis PDF itself is not published with the corpus (it is kept with the development repository).
* Words the thesis glosses as one unit are one word in the word tier (FormosanBank POL-060): the thesis writes a clitic as its own token (`Ini =ku`) but usually prints one gloss for host and clitic (`不-我`), so those 95 pairs are single words. The sentence text is unchanged.
* In one sentence (`asu ∅-skiya`) FormosanBank added the `∅` the thesis's gloss implies; a note on that sentence says so.
