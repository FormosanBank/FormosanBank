# djavadjavay

djavadjavay a mapuljat imaza itua wanglu i FormosanBank, a pinenetjan mavan a kidjumak tua milimilingan a sengsengan. izuanan a nia sinivarung tu semeqetj sa paljazua tua tja kai se kacalisian i Taiwan. aicu a mareka kinakaian namasan pazangal aravac tucu itua pinenetj a kai na senatau, ljakua tucu na secevung tua gemagalj, aza masanitalj izua uri maqulip a gemagaljan a munday paru tjamapulju anga sikataqaljan a saigu a qivu. avansika aza tja sengsengan imaza mavan a lemingdjelj tua kikai sa maqati a kemeljang tu namakuda a macekaw ca nia vinecikan, nu tjaivililj, teveljeng a kinkiw, patulu katua papigacal a saliman.

imaza itua FormosanBank, maqati sun a kemin tua pakata aicu a qinepu kata pinalimasudj a 17 a kemacalisian a vecik a pinakeljang. a kemeljang, sa aicu a milimilingan izuanga napaselaw tu 8,219,206 a vecik katua 702.9 a tukian ta zaing a ziriu. ([tja pacunan ta ziriu pakamaza](https://ai4commsci.gitbook.io/formosanbank/the-bank-architecture/corpora)). utauta dikilu a kemeljang aicu a vinecikan tu kemuda a cemekaw kata semeqetj a sinisiukay：pakatua penuljat a tja sengsengan katua milimilingan itua [GitHub](https://github.com/FormosanBank/FormosanBank) a sivecikan. saka aza penuljat a zaing a ziriu sineqetj a pasamaza itua [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/FormosanBank).
